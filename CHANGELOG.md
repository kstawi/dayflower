# Changelog

Each version's section here becomes its GitHub Release notes, and the notes shown with the update (see
`.github/workflows/release.yml`).

## 0.7.0

The first release you can install.

- **Several Google accounts in one calendar:** day, week, month and agenda views, with meetings that are on two of
  your accounts shown once.
- **Join:** a Join button on every video meeting (Google Meet, Zoom, Teams, Webex and more), in the event's details,
  on the event in the day and week views shortly before it starts, and on reminders.
- **Reminders** that match the ones you set in Google Calendar, with Snooze and a "starting now" alert.
- **Editing:** create, change and delete events, including repeating ones, drag to move or resize, reply to
  invitations, and see guests' availability.
- **Search** across all your accounts, a notification with Accept / Maybe / Decline when an invitation arrives, and a
  world clock in the side pane.
- **Starts with Windows** and keeps running in the background for reminders; both can be turned off in Settings,
  and Quit is there too.
- **Updates itself** from GitHub: updates download in the background and install the next time Dayflower starts, or
  at once from Settings.

The installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows may show "Windows protected your PC": choose **More info**, then
**Run anyway**. Signing in with Google shows an "unverified app" warning until Google has reviewed Dayflower.
